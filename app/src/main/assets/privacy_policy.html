Overview

The app processes media links on your device to download media you own or have permission to save. Download history and settings are stored locally on the device.

Browser sessions

If you use the in-app browser, session cookies remain in Android WebView storage. When required for a download, the app may create a short-lived cookie file in private app cache and deletes it after the job completes or is cancelled.

Network access

The app connects to the media URL, yt-dlp update sources, and the websites you choose to open. The app does not sell personal information.

Files

Completed files are published to the public Downloads/Download Anything folder. Temporary work files remain in app-specific storage until completion or cleanup.
